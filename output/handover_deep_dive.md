profit per deal**. A screening tool valued every local listing; the properties below are the ones worth a closer
look. Please go through the **priority properties** one by one and:

2. **Check the refurb budget** against what the listing shows (kitchen, bathroom, heating, rewire, roof, damp,
   extensions, garden) and give your own figure.
3. **Re-work the numbers** with any corrected value and refurb: profit at asking, and the max offer that still
   leaves £40k.
4. **Flag deal-breakers or big unknowns:** construction type, tenure/lease, tenants, knotweed, flood, subsidence,
   planning, auction terms, Modern Method of Auction reservation fees, short-dated auctions, bungalows valued from
   house sales, portal property types that look wrong.
5. **Give a verdict for each** (pursue / pursue at £X / pass) and the questions to ask the agent. End with the
   ranked top 5.

Then skim the **second-tier table** and name any that deserve promoting. Treat every figure as a starting point to
challenge, not a fact. Where the listing contradicts the data, the listing wins.

## How the numbers were produced

- **After-works value:** HM Land Registry sales (24–36 months, same type, within 0.5–1 mile) matched to EPC floor areas; only sales within ±25% of the subject's size (±35% if thin), £/sq ft outliers removed, weighted by distance and recency. Low / base / high = 50th / 70th / 85th percentile £/sq ft x subject sq ft (a refurbished house should sell in the upper part of a mixed-condition sample).
- **Floor area:** from the listing (ignored if under 450 sq ft for 2+ beds); otherwise estimated from nearby same-type EPCs, capped by bedrooms (1 bed 600, 2 bed 800, 3 bed 1,000), confidence LOW.
- **Confidence:** MEDIUM = 8+ size-matched sales, low-high spread <= 20%, real floor area, and estimated value no more than 2x the price. Nothing has been independently valued.
- **Refurb:** £35/sq ft modernise, £55/sq ft derelict/poor, min £15,000; contingency 10% of refurb.
- **Costs:** SDLT at additional-dwelling rates (+5%); buy legal £1,500 + survey £600; holding £350/month for 6 months; agent 1.44% incl. VAT + sale legal £1,200; cash purchase, no finance.
- **Auctions:** guide + 25%; Modern Method of Auction adds a 5.4% reservation fee incl. VAT (min £6,000).
- **Verdicts:** CALL AGENT = asking at or below max offer, MEDIUM confidence. INVESTIGATE = at or below max offer with LOW confidence, or up to £15k above it.
