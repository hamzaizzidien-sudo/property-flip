4. **Flag deal-breakers or big unknowns:** construction type, tenure/lease, tenants, knotweed, flood, subsidence, planning, auction terms, MMoA reservation fees, short-dated auctions.
5. **Give a verdict for each one** (pursue / pursue at £X / pass) and the questions to ask the agent. End with the ranked top 3.

Treat every figure below as a starting point to challenge, not a fact. Where the listing contradicts the data, the listing wins.

## How the numbers were produced

- **After-works value:** HM Land Registry sales (last 24–36 months, same property type, within 0.5–1 mile), matched to EPC floor areas. Only sales within ±25% of the subject's size are kept (±35% if that leaves too few), £/sq ft outliers are removed, and sales are weighted by distance and recency. Low / base / high = the 50th / 70th / 85th percentile £/sq ft × the subject's sq ft. The idea is that a refurbished house should sell in the upper part of a mixed-condition sample.
- **Floor area:** from the listing where it gives one. Otherwise it's estimated from nearby same-type EPCs, capped by bedrooms (1 bed 600, 2 bed 800, 3 bed 1,000 sq ft), and confidence is set to LOW.
- **Confidence:** MEDIUM = 8+ size-matched sales and a low-to-high spread of 20% or less. LOW otherwise, or whenever the size is estimated. Nothing has been independently valued.
- **Refurb:** £35/sq ft to modernise, £55/sq ft for derelict or poor condition, minimum £15,000; contingency 10% of refurb.
- **Costs:** SDLT at the additional-dwelling rates (+5% surcharge); buy legal £1,500 + survey £600; holding £350/month for 6 months; agent 1.44% incl. VAT + sale legal £1,200; cash purchase, no finance costs.
- **Auctions:** traditional auction lots assume a price of guide + 25%. Modern Method of Auction adds a buyer reservation fee of 5.4% incl. VAT (minimum £6,000).
- **Verdict bands:** CALL AGENT = asking at or below max offer with MEDIUM confidence. INVESTIGATE = asking at or below max offer with LOW confidence, or up to £15k above it.
